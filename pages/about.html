About ViewIT

Your all-in-one platform for discovering movies and TV shows.

What is ViewIT?

ViewIT is a web-based application designed to help users discover upcoming and recently released movies and TV shows in one simple and accessible platform.

Instead of visiting multiple websites to find release dates, ratings, and availability, ViewIT brings this information together using reliable data from the TMDB API.

Our Aim

The aim of ViewIT is to provide a clean, ad-free, and easy-to-use experience for film and TV fans who want quick access to accurate and up-to-date information.
